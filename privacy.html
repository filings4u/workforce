Depending on your relationship with screenings4u, we may process account identifiers, business contact information, organization data, employee and testing workflow information, billing information, support communications, technical logs, and documents you choose to store in the platform.

We use administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and retain records according to operational, contractual, legal, and compliance requirements.
