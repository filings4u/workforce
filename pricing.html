Pricing questions

Software access is separate from testing fulfillment.

Do I have to purchase drug or alcohol tests from screenings4u?

No. The platform is sold as software. You can manage testing performed by other providers and optionally purchase screenings4u testing services when convenient.

Are the listed prices monthly?

Yes. All nine plans shown above are monthly software subscription prices.

Are testing fees included in the subscription?

No. Drug tests, alcohol tests, collections, MRO services, and other fulfillment are separate transactions unless a future bundled offer explicitly says otherwise.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. The subscription architecture is designed so plan entitlements can be changed while keeping the same organization and account data.
