Pricing questions

Software access is separate from testing fulfillment.

Do I need an account before checkout?

No. Workforce subscriptions are guest-first. After payment, you complete company setup and receive your secure portal invitation.

Are testing fees included?

No. Drug and alcohol testing services are separate purchases available from inside the authenticated Workforce portal.

Where are DOT / Owner-Operator plans?

DOT and Owner-Operator products are managed separately at dot.screenings4u.com.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Your organization data stays with the account while plan entitlements change.
