Pricing questions

Software access is separate from testing fulfillment.

Do I need an account before checkout?

No. Workforce subscriptions are guest-first. After payment, you complete company setup and receive your secure portal invitation.

Do I choose my plan again during checkout?

No. The plan you click on this page is carried directly into checkout and locked for that purchase.

Are drug and alcohol testing fees included?

No. Testing services are separate purchases available from inside your authenticated Workforce portal.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Your organization data stays with the account while plan entitlements change.
